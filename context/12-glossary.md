# 12 — Glossary (plain English)

For the glossary slide (appendix A1). One line each.

- **Vendor** — An outside company that sells software to a company. Example: Trissino.
- **Integration** — A connection that lets one piece of software read data from another.
- **API** — The "door" software uses to talk to other software.
- **OAuth** — The "allow this app" button. It creates a key that lets the app read data.
- **Scope / permission** — What the key is allowed to see or do.
- **Admin-level app** — One admin says yes for the whole company. The app can see everyone.
- **User-level app** — Each person says yes for themselves only.
- **Bring your own OAuth** — The company uses its own app keys instead of ours.
  It keeps more control.
- **Proxy** — Something that stands in the middle and passes messages along.
  Gatekeeper is a proxy that also filters.
- **Audit log** — A record of every request: who asked, for what, and what was sent.
- **IT / security team** — The people in a company who decide which tools can access data.
- **MCP** — A standard way for AI assistants to plug into other software.
- **Breach** — When attackers get data they should not have.
